Knee Load

A browser-only squat analyser. It tracks a side-on video, measures knee flexion and estimates tibiofemoral compression in body weights — no server, no upload, no app install. One <div> and one script tag.

It reports knee flexion, knee compression, quadriceps force and the shank moment arm, plus rolling charts, rep peaks and a CSV export. Everything runs on the device; video never leaves the browser.

Drop it anywhere

The loader mounts a responsive iframe, so host styles cannot collide with the widget. 

<div id="pose"></div>
<script src="https://imadrian.fyi/pose-widget/embed.js"></script>

Camera access requires HTTPS. For a live demo, use a laptop webcam or a phone in landscape on a tripod, side-on with your whole body in frame.

How it works

  1. Pose. MediaPipe BlazePose returns 33 landmarks. The widget filters them and maps them onto the COCO-17 skeleton used by the Python demo.
  2. Calibrate. Your height gives pixels-per-metre through anthropometric segment ratios. Stand tall for three seconds; then the scale is locked.
  3. Physics. Ground reaction × shank lever arm gives knee torque. Divided by the patellar tendon arm, it gives quadriceps and joint compression.

What it reports

  • Knee flexion in degrees.
  • Knee compression in body weights and newtons.
  • Quadriceps force in body weights.
  • Shank moment arm, vertical ground reaction force per leg and whole-body COM acceleration.
  • Rolling 12-second charts, rep count, rep period and per-rep peak load.

The widget follows this page's light/dark toggle. An embed can force a theme with data-theme="light" or data-theme="dark".

Estimates for teaching and relative comparison. Not a force plate and not medical advice. 2D side view only; external load is not modelled.
